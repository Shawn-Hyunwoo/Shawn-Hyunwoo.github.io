Professional Summary

Quantum algorithm researcher on the Quantum AI Team at Norma, working on practical and experimentally realizable quantum algorithms for the NISQ era. Interested in hardware-efficient quantum machine learning, Pauli-propagation methods, noise-resilient variational quantum circuits (VQCs), and quantum generative models for drug discovery.
