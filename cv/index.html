Professional Summary

Research scientist on the Quantum AI Team at Norma, working on practical quantum algorithms for the NISQ era. Interested in classical simulation of quantum circuits and the quantum–classical boundary (Pauli propagation, classical surrogates), quantum machine learning and quantum generative models, HPC–QPU hybrid workflows, and hardware-aware NISQ algorithms and quantum error mitigation. B.S. in Physics & Artificial Intelligence from Sogang University.

Experience

  • 2025 - present

    Seoul, South Korea

    Research Scientist, Quantum AI Team
    Norma, Inc.
    • Co-developed PADO-Pauli (arXiv:2610.17755), an open-source, differentiable Pauli-propagation engine in PyTorch: a circuit is compiled once into sparse tensor maps, then evaluated and back-propagated in batch on CUDA and ROCm GPUs. Zero filtering removes propagated Pauli terms that cannot contribute to the result (~1.5x faster batched evaluation, 1.4-3.0x lower steady-state memory).
    • Against NVIDIA cuPauliProp 0.4.0 on one A100 80 GB: 2.7-10.1x faster evaluation, 3.3-13.3x faster forward and backward passes, 206x higher throughput on batched inputs, and 3.1x less GPU memory at 16 qubits (14.5 vs. 45.4 GB), completing a setting in which cuPauliProp ran out of device memory.
    • Reproduced IBM’s 127-qubit kicked-Ising utility experiment on one AMD MI300X with under 0.7 GB of GPU memory (RMS deviation 0.032 from the hardware data); extended the engine with a hybrid CPU-storage / GPU-compute mode, prototyped multi-GPU execution on an 8-GPU NVIDIA Blackwell node, and ported Pauli-propagation simulation to the Tenstorrent Blackhole p150a NPU.
    • Co-first author of SAFE ma-QAOA (IEEE QCE 2026): Pauli-propagation pre-training, parameter distillation and exact fine-tuning achieved better solution quality than standard training while reducing estimated QPU workload by ~95% on average.
    • Co-developed mixed IQP-QCBMs, ancilla-augmented mixtures of IQP circuits for generative modeling, benchmarked from 16 to 784 qubits.
    • Sole developer of a latent-space quantum GAN for KRAS G12D inhibitor discovery (National Research Foundation of Korea), trained on a Transformer latent space built from 1.4M augmented SMILES.
    • Built a neural-network quantum error mitigation (QEM) model that reduced the total variation distance (TVD) to ideal outputs; studied VQE and QAOA noise on IBM, IonQ, and Quantinuum hardware.
